## Prompt 1

Following `skills/scheme-dev/SKILL.md`, start `code/82_q3.rkt` with `#lang racket/base`.
Implement ONLY the accumulator-based O(n) helpers `inorder-list`, `preorder-list`, and `postorder-list` for binary trees `()` or `(value left right)`.
Use strictly allowed built-ins (no `append`, `reverse`, `map`, `first`, `rest`, `empty?`). Use right-to-left accumulator consing so all three traversals build their lists in exact forward order with zero `reverse`, and verify against `t1 = '(4 (2 (1 () ()) (3 () ())) (6 (5 () ()) (7 () ())))` that they yield `(1 2 3 4 5 6 7)`, `(4 2 1 3 6 5 7)`, and `(1 3 2 5 7 6 4)`. No top-level calls or prints.

## Prompt 3

Use strictly allowed built-ins only—do NOT use `values` or `let-values` (if returning both from one helper, use `cons`/`car`/`cdr`, or write two clean recursive helpers).
